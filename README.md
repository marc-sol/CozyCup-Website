## About Us

At **CozyCup**, we believe in creating a warm, inviting atmosphere where you can relax, recharge, and enjoy life’s little pleasures. Our mission is to provide high-quality beverages, freshly made snacks, and a welcoming space for you to unwind.

We source our ingredients locally, ensuring each cup is brewed to perfection, and every bite is fresh and delicious.
